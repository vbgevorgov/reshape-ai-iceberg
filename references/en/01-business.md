# Stage 1/5 — Business Outcomes

Identify the current decision, its owner, the workflow without the proposed AI, and the business outcome that would have to improve for the product to be worth operating. Define the primary KPI separately from guardrail metrics and cost of a wrong decision. A before/after comparison without controls does not establish causation.

Find two domain-specific observations that could change the initial solution. For each show `observation or hypothesis → consequence → verification → decision affected`. Do not count generic remarks about data quality as discoveries.

**Guided:** ask at most one decisive question in this message and at most four in the whole journey, only if the answer can change objective, risk boundaries, or strategy. **Autonomous:** state realistic but hypothetical goals and continue with no questions.

Output: decision, KPI and guardrails, error cost, effect-evaluation design, evidence status.
